## 📌 Research Overvie
This repository contains the complete computational pipeline, datasets, and empirical results for the study investigating the impact of **Artificial Linguistic Veneer (ALV)** and **AI Agency** on **Oral Accountability (ODP)** among postgraduate EFL learners.

**Core Research Question:** How does the perceived agency of AI tools moderate the relationship between the use of linguistic veneers and the learner's accountability for their academic output?
